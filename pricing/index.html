A few things to know

What am I paying for?

Ada Engine is free and open source. Lite, Pro and Max are subscriptions to Ada Cloud services with different monthly AI credit allowances. Publishing limits are the same across all paid plans. Your games and assets remain yours.

How does billing work?

Cloud Lite — 250 ₽, Cloud Pro — 500 ₽, Cloud Max — 1,000 ₽. Prices are per month, with no trial period. Automatic renewal requires your consent. The total, billing period and next charge date are shown before payment.

Can I cancel my subscription?

You can turn off renewal and keep your paid plan until the end of the paid period. For subscriptions purchased in the App Store, manage renewal through Apple. For web subscriptions, contact support@adaengine.org.

How do refunds work?

You may request a full refund of your first paid Cloud subscription within seven days if you have not published builds. This extra guarantee does not limit your statutory rights. Contact support@adaengine.org with your account and purchase ID; never send card details.

What happens when I reach a limit?

There are no automatic overage charges. Publishing traffic pauses when the monthly allowance runs out. Publication time limits continue to run.
